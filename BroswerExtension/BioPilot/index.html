The problem

Bad reagents and irreproducible results cost months

Misidentified cell lines, non-specific antibodies, and experiments that simply don't replicate are hidden in the literature. BioPilot surfaces them while you read — and lets the community flag them for everyone after you.

🧫

Reagent risk flags, automatically

BioPilot detects antibodies, cell lines, and plasmids by RRID or catalog number, and shows a hover card with official details and warnings — like cell lines flagged by ICLAC as misidentified or cross-contaminated. Avoid the landmine before you order.

💬

Structured, verified commenting

Anchor a comment to any passage and sort it into research-relevant categories. No free-for-all threads — every comment carries a clear, high-signal label that other scientists can trust.

🪪

Trust you can verify

Comments can carry a verified ORCID ✓ badge and institution. Prefer privacy? Post anonymously — you're still authenticated behind the scenes, so there's no spam, but your name stays hidden.
