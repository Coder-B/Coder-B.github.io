Core Features
Everything you need to read papers with confidence

BioPilot layers peer-review intelligence on top of every biomedical paper you read — no copy-paste, no tab-switching.

🔬

Reagent Auto-Tagging P0

Automatically identifies antibody catalogue numbers (Cat#), cell line names, and RRIDs in PubMed, Nature, and Cell pages. Hover to see provenance, usage frequency, and risk flags from ICLAC and Cellosaurus.

💬

Structured Commenting P0

Attach peer comments categorised as Missing detail, Reagent anomaly, Image concern, or Reproduced. Identity verified via ORCID or institutional email — transparent but pseudonymous.

🔗

PubPeer Integration P0

See how many PubPeer discussions exist for any paper — displayed inline next to the title. No more manually searching another site; all concerns surface where you already are.

🖼

AI Image Consistency Check P1

Select two Western blot bands or micrograph panels; BioPilot runs a similarity check in the background and highlights potential duplications, crops, or suspicious pixel patterns.

📋

Protocol Parser P1

Dense Methods sections are re-structured into clean step-by-step checklists by an LLM, making it instantly obvious what was — and wasn't — reported.

🏆

Contributor Dashboard

Track your own annotations, up-votes, and reproducibility tags. Download a verifiable academic contribution record — a new currency for open-science credit.

How It Works
Five seconds from open to insight
1

Open any biomedical paper

Navigate to a PubMed abstract, a Nature/Cell/Science article, or a preprint. BioPilot activates silently in the background.

2

Silent scan

The extension scans the full text for catalogue numbers, RRIDs, DOIs, and known cell-line names in milliseconds — no page reload required.

3

Visual overlays appear

Risky reagents get a red underline; verified ones get green. A bubble in the margin shows how many PubPeer comments exist. Everything is subtle — it doesn't get in your way.

4

Hover or click for detail

Hover over any tagged reagent for its identity card. Click the BioPilot icon in the toolbar to trigger the AI image-consistency audit on the current page.

5

Contribute back

Spotted a missing protocol step or a suspicious figure? Add a structured comment in two clicks. Your ORCID ties it to your scholarly identity — no anonymous trolling.
