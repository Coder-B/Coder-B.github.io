What Roomery Does

Roomery turns any room photo into a renovation preview. Snap a photo of a space and instantly see it with new flooring, fresh paint, tile, and finishes — powered by AI — then build a cost estimate and share a polished before & after with clients.

Features

Snap & Transform

Take a photo of any room and let AI re-render it with the materials and colors you choose.

Edit by Area

Tap the region you want to change — floor, walls, ceiling, and more — and apply a material, color, or finish to just that area.

Layered Changes

Stack edits to redesign a whole space step by step, and revert any change instantly if you change your mind.

Cost Estimates

Add quantities, unit prices, and a waste buffer per area, plus extras like labor and permits, for a clear running total.

Share With Clients

Generate a before & after link with the full estimate that anyone can open in a browser — no app required.

How to Use

  • Start a project: Tap Snap a Photo on the home screen and take or pick a room photo.
  • Make a change: Tap an area in the photo, choose a material/color/finish, and generate the result.
  • Build on it: Keep applying changes to layer your design; tap revert to undo the last change.
  • Estimate costs: Open the estimate, fill in quantities and prices, and add any extra line items.
  • Share: Tap share to create a link you can send to clients or save for your records.

FAQ

Why didn't my room generate any editable areas?

Use a clear, well-lit photo taken straight on. Very dark, blurry, or extreme-angle shots can make it harder to detect areas. Try retaking the photo or starting a new project.

How many room generations do I get?

Each device starts with a free allowance of AI generations. Your remaining count is shown at the top of the home screen.

A generation failed or looks wrong — did it use my allowance?

No. Your allowance is only used on a successful generation. If a result isn't what you wanted, revert it and try a different material or photo.

How do I share an estimate with a client?

Open a project and tap share. Roomery creates a web link with your before & after images and cost estimate that opens in any browser.

Can I recover a deleted project?

Deleting a project archives it rather than erasing it. Contact us at the email below if you need a project restored.

How is my data handled?

Photos you upload are used to generate your renovation previews and are stored securely so you can revisit your projects. We do not sell your data. See our privacy policy for details.
