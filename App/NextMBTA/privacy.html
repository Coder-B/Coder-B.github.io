NextMBTA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

NextMBTA ("the App") is built and maintained by Coder-B. This page explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. We do not require an account or login.

Location Data

The App may request access to your device's location solely to show nearby stops and provide relevant transit predictions. Location data is used on-device only and is never transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The App fetches real-time transit data from the MBTA public API. No personal data is sent as part of these requests. Please review the MBTA's own privacy policy for details on their data practices.

Analytics

The App does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
