Why it feels hard

The difficult part isn't pressing play.
It's knowing how play ends.

“One more video” turns into a negotiation when the stopping point feels sudden or keeps moving. Gentle Bye gives the session a shape a child can understand: what we're watching, how long we have, and what happens next.

How it works

A clear beginning, middle, and goodbye.

Parents set up the session. Children get a predictable experience.

  1. 01
    ▶︎

    Choose together

    Search for public YouTube videos or paste a link. Review the choices and arrange up to 20 videos.

  2. 02

Parent-led by design

You choose the content.
Gentle Bye holds the boundary.

Gentle Bye is a transition tool, not an automatic babysitter or a content recommendation service. A parent selects every video and starts each session.

  • Strict SafeSearch is used for video search
  • Parent controls are protected with biometrics or a simple challenge
  • No child profile, name, email, or password is required

Automated filters are not perfect. Parents should review every video before adding it.

Good to know

Questions parents ask

Straight answers about how Gentle Bye works.

What is Gentle Bye?

Gentle Bye is an iPhone and iPad app that helps a parent choose public YouTube videos, set one total viewing limit, and prepare a child for a calm transition when the session ends.

Does it require an account?

No. Gentle Bye does not require a name, email address, or password. The app uses an anonymous installation identifier to operate sessions.

Does it decide what my child watches?

No. A parent searches for, reviews, and selects every video. Search uses YouTube's strict SafeSearch setting, but parents remain responsible for choosing appropriate content.

How do parents leave Kids Mode?

Parent controls use Face ID or Touch ID. If biometric authentication is unavailable or canceled, Gentle Bye provides a simple addition challenge for the parent.
