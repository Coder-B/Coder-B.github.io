Questions

Before you start.

What is Afterlook?

Afterlook is an iPhone app that transforms room photos into AI renovation previews and helps homeowners and contractors estimate and share a project before work begins.

Can I edit one part of a room?

Yes. Select a detected area such as the floor, walls or ceiling, then apply a material, color or finish to that area. You can layer multiple changes on the same room.

Can clients view a project without the app?

Yes. Afterlook can create a shareable before-and-after page with the project estimate that opens in a web browser.

How accurate is an AI renovation preview?

An AI renovation preview is a visual planning aid, not a construction drawing or guarantee of the finished result. Lighting, material appearance, dimensions, installation details and real-world conditions can differ.

Does Afterlook provide a contractor quote?

No. Afterlook helps you organize quantities, unit prices, labor, permits, contingency and other costs, but the result is a planning estimate based on your inputs—not a professional quote.

What happens to my room photos?

Photos are processed to generate renovation previews and stored so you can revisit projects. Read the privacy policy for details and deletion instructions.
